About Aggie Engagement

Aggie Engagement serves as the connection point between student-athletes, alumni, employers, and the community. Through leadership development, alumni engagement, strategic partnerships, and service initiatives, we create opportunities that support Aggies during their time at Utah State and long after graduation.

Our Programs

Pathways for Every Part of the Aggie Network

Aggies Lead

Aggies Lead is Utah State Athletics' student-athlete development program, focused on leadership, career readiness, personal growth, and life beyond sport. Through workshops, mentorship, service, and professional development opportunities, Aggies Lead prepares student-athletes to succeed during their time at Utah State and after graduation.

Leadership Development Career Readiness Professional Growth Community Engagement

A-Club

A-Club connects former Utah State student-athletes back to the teams, people, and traditions that shaped their Aggie experience. Through alumni events, networking opportunities, mentorship, and continued engagement, A-Club helps athletics alumni stay connected to Utah State for life.

Alumni Networking Mentorship Signature Events Lifelong Connection

Community Outreach

Community Outreach creates meaningful opportunities for student-athletes to serve, connect, and make an impact beyond competition. Through service initiatives, youth engagement, school partnerships, and local outreach, Aggie Engagement strengthens the relationship between Utah State Athletics and the surrounding community.

Service Initiatives Youth Engagement Community Impact Civic Leadership

Strategic Partnerships

Strategic Partnerships connect businesses, employers, and community organizations with Utah State student-athletes and athletics initiatives. These partnerships create opportunities for internships, career exploration, sponsorships, professional development, and meaningful collaboration across the Aggie network.

Employer Partnerships Internship Opportunities Career Connections Strategic Collaboration
